# Faculty Entrepreneurship and Technical Start-ups Policy (Revision 2026)

## 1. Scope
This revision replaces the 2022 policy for every faculty member, researcher and
student team that forms a technical start-up using university facilities, funding,
laboratories or intellectual property.

## 2. University Equity Stake
The university retains an equity stake of 10% in a start-up that uses only incubation
space and mentoring, and up to 30% when university-owned intellectual property or
laboratory equipment is licensed to the start-up. The 2022 range of 15% to 50% no longer applies.

## 3. Disclosure and Approval
Faculty founders must disclose the venture to the Office of Research, Innovation and
Commercialization within 30 days of incorporation. The Dean approves any arrangement
in which a faculty member spends more than one day per week on start-up work.

## 4. Conflict of Interest
A faculty founder may not supervise, grade or assess a student who is employed by,
or holds equity in, the same start-up.
